Projects

F1 DRS Upper Wing: Aerodynamic Analysis

ME 3330 Fluid Mechanics Lab, Yaşar University, 2025 to 2026

Three scaled NACA wing profiles, designed in Fusion 360, 3D printed and tested in the T-490 open-circuit wind tunnel to compare lift, drag and flow behavior.

  • Fusion 360
  • 3D printing
  • Wind tunnel testing
  • Aerodynamics
Method and details
  • Sized the models to the T-490 test section, with a calculated blockage ratio of 7%.
  • Printed the models with FDM and finished the surfaces with 800-grit wet sandpaper to improve flow quality.
  • Measured lift (FL) and drag (FD) forces at multiple angles of attack.
  • Calculated lift and drag coefficients (CL, CD) and analyzed flow separation and vortex formation with smoke visualization.
  • Compared the Strouhal number (St ≈ 0.2) with the theoretical value and evaluated the three profiles against each other.
